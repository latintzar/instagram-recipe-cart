# Social Recipes illustration

This explanatory diagram uses Vitrina's original recipe-phone and grocery-bag drawings, retained in its angel deck as `doodle-say-clean.png` and `doodle-stretch-clean.png`. The grocery bag is isolated from the latter with an alpha mask; low-opacity paper texture is removed from the drawings. The Instagram mark is the retained mark from the same deck. The composition and animated connector explain the Instagram reel → recipe → local grocery basket workflow; they are not a screenshot of an Instagram conversation or a model-generated product interface.
